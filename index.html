1. Police & General Complaints

Mobile Khone Ki Police FIR / Complaint

Police station me khoye hue mobile phone ki report likhwane ke liye application.

Seva me, Shri maan Thana Adhyaksh Ji, [Police Station ka Naam], [City Name] Vishay: Mobile Phone kho jaane ke sambandh me application. Mahoday, Nivedan hai ki mera naam [Aapka Naam] hai. Main [Aapka Pura Pata] ka niwasi hoon. Dinaank [Tareekh] ko samay lagbhag [Samay] baje mera mobile phone [Jagah ka naam] ke paas kho gaya hai. Kaafi khojbeen ke baad bhi mobile nahi mila. Mobile ki details: 1. Mobile Brand & Model: [Samsung / Redmi etc.] 2. Mobile Number (SIM): [Aapka Number] 3. IMEI Number 1: [15 Digit IMEI] 4. IMEI Number 2: [15 Digit IMEI] Atah aapse nivedan hai ki kripya meri yeh shikayat darj karne ki kripa karein. Dhananyawad. Bhavdiya, Naam: [Aapka Naam] Phone Number: [Aapka Number] Pata: [Aapka Pata] Dinaank: [Date]

Driving License (DL) Loss Application

RTO ya Police station me duplicate DL ya FIR ke liye application.

Seva me, Shri maan Thana Adhyaksh Ji, [Police Station Name], [City] Vishay: Driving License kho jaane ki Soochna hetu. Mahoday, Savinay nivedan hai ki mera naam [Aapka Naam] hai. Mera Original Driving License (DL Number: [DL Number]) dinaank [Tareekh] ko [Location] ke paas kahin gir gaya hai. Atah aapse prarthna hai ki kripya iski missing report darj karke mujhe copy pradaan karein. Dhananyawad. Naam: [Aapka Naam] Mobile: [Mobile Number] Dinaank: [Date]

2. Banking Applications

Bank Account Branch Transfer Application

Apne bank account ko ek branch se doosri branch me transfer karwane ke liye application.

Seva me, Shri maan Shaakha Prabandhak (Branch Manager), [Bank Ka Naam, Jaise: State Bank of India], [Purani Branch Ka Naam aur Pata] Vishay: Bank Account doosri Branch me Transfer karwane hetu. Mahoday, Savinay nivedan hai ki mera aapki branch me ek bachat khata (Savings Account) hai, jiska account number [Aapka Account Number] hai. Mera niwas sthan badal jaane ke kaaran ab mujhe purani branch se lenden karne me asuvidha hoti hai. Main apna khata aapki dusri branch [Nayi Branch Ka Naam aur IFSC Code] me transfer karwana chahta/chahti hoon. Khate ki jankari: Khata Dharak ka Naam: [Aapka Naam] Account Number: [Account Number] Nayi Branch Ka Naam: [Nayi Branch] Nayi Branch Code/IFSC: [IFSC Code] Atah aapse nivedan hai ki mere khate ko jald se jald nayi branch me transfer karne ki kripa karein. Dhananyawad. Bhavdiya, Naam: [Aapka Naam] Account No: [Account Number] Mobile No: [Mobile Number] Dinaank: [Date]

New Passbook Request Application

Passbook bhar jaane ya kho jaane par nayi passbook lene ke liye application.

Seva me, Shri maan Shaakha Prabandhak, [Bank Ka Naam], [Branch Name] Vishay: Nayi Passbook jaari karne hetu application. Mahoday, Mera naam [Aapka Naam] hai aur mera account number [Account Number] aapke bank me hai. Meri purani passbook [Poori bhar chuki hai / Kho gayi hai]. Mujhe aage ke lenden ke liye nayi passbook ki aavashyakta hai. Atah aapse nivedan hai ki mujhe nayi passbook jaari karne ki kripa karein. Dhananyawad. Bhavdiya, Naam: [Aapka Naam] Account No: [Account Number] Mobile: [Mobile Number] Dinaank: [Date]

Bank Account Closure Application

Purana ya unused bank account band karwane ke liye application.

Seva me, Shri maan Shaakha Prabandhak, [Bank Ka Naam], [Branch Name] Vishay: Bank Account Band (Close) karwane hetu. Mahoday, Nivedan hai ki mera aapke bank me bachat khata number [Account Number] hai. [Kaaran, jaise: Kisi vyaktigat kaaran se / Khate ka istemal na hone se] main is khate ko aage nahi chalana chahta/chahti hoon. Atah aapse nivedan hai ki mere is bank account ko band karke isme bachi hui rashi [Cash de dein / Dusre account me bhej dein]. Khata Dharak Ka Naam: [Aapka Naam] Account Number: [Account Number] Dhananyawad. Bhavdiya, Naam: [Aapka Naam] Mobile: [Mobile Number] Dinaank: [Date]

3. Public Utilities & Revenue (Zameen/Bijli)

Electricity Meter Faulty / Bill Correction Application

Bijli ka meter kharab hone ya galat bill aane par bijli vibhag ko application.

Seva me, Shri maan Sahayak Abhiyanta (Assistant Engineer), Vidhyut Vitran Vibhag (Electricity Department), [Aapke ilake ka naam, City] Vishay: Kharab Bijli Meter badalne / Bill Sahi karne hetu. Mahoday, Savinay nivedan hai ki main [Aapka Naam], [Aapka Pata] ka niwasi hoon. Mera Consumer Number / Meter Connection Number [Consumer Number] hai. Pichle kuch dino se mera bijli ka meter [Bohot tez chal raha hai / Band ho gaya hai / Galat reading de raha hai], jis wajah se mera bill bohot zyada aaya hai. Atah aapse prarthna hai ki kripya meter ki jaanch karwa kar ise badalne ya bill ko sahi karne ki kripa karein. Dhananyawad. Bhavdiya, Naam: [Aapka Naam] Consumer Number: [Consumer ID] Mobile: [Mobile Number] Dinaank: [Date]

Zameen Napai / Land Survey Application (Revenue Dept)

Zameen ki sar सरकारी napai (demarcation) karwane ke liye CO/Tehsildar ko application.

Seva me, Shri maan Tehsildar / Anumandaliya Adhikari (CO) Ji, [Tehsil / Anumandal Ka Naam], [District Name] Vishay: Zameen ki sarkari napai (Demarcation) karwane hetu. Mahoday, Nivedan hai ki main [Aapka Naam], Gram: [Gaon/Mohalla], Thana: [Thana], Zila: [District] ka sthayi niwasi hoon. Meri katha/khesra sankhya ki zameen ki jankari niche di gayi hai: 1. Khata Number: [Khata No] 2. Khesra / Plot Number: [Khesra No] 3. Rakba (Area): [Kitni Zameen Hai] Mera padosi se seema ko lekar vivad ho raha hai, isliye main apni zameen ki sarkari amin dwara napai karwana chahta hoon. Sarkari shulk jama karne ko taiyar hoon. Atah aapse nivedan hai ki kripya Amin ko aadesh dekar zameen ki napai karwane ki kripa karein. Dhananyawad. Aavedak, Naam: [Aapka Naam] Pata: [Aapka Pata] Mobile No: [Mobile Number] Dinaank: [Date]
