1. Police Station FIR & Criminal Cases

Maar-Peet / Physical Assault FIR Complaint

Aahata, maar-peet aur jaan se maarne ki dhamki ki FIR darj karwane hetu format.

Seva me, Shri maan Thana Adhyaksh Ji, [Police Station ka Naam], [Zila/City Name] Vishay: Maar-peet, gaali-galoch aur jaan se maarne ki dhamki dene ke sambandh me FIR darj karne hetu. Mahoday, Savinay nivedan hai ki mera naam [Aapka Naam] hai. Main [Aapka Pura Pata] ka niwasi hoon. Dinaank [Date] ko samay lagbhag [Time] baje main [Jagah ka Naam] par tha. Tabhi achanak [Aropi ka Naam / Agyat log] aaye aur bina kisi wajah ke mujhe gaali-galoch karne lage. Jab maine virodh kiya toh unhone [Lathi/Danda/Pistol/Dharadar Hathyar] se mujh par jaanleva hamla kar diya. Is hamle me mujhe [Shareer ke ang jahan chot aayi] par gambhir chot aayi hai aur unhone mujhe jaan se maarne ki dhamki bhi di hai. Ghatna ke samay wahan [Gawahon ke naam] maujood the jinhone mujhe bachaya. Atah aapse nivedan hai ki kripya ukt aropiyon ke khilaf uchit dharaon (IPC/BNS) ke tehat FIR darj kar kanooni karwai karne ki kripa karein aur mera Medical Examination karwayein. Dhananyawad. Aavedak, Naam: [Aapka Naam] Mobile: [Mobile Number] Pata: [Aapka Pata] Dinaank: [Date]

Financial Fraud / Online Scam Complaint

Paisa dhokhadhadi, online scam ya fake scheme me paise doobne par police me shikayat format.

Seva me, Shri maan Thana Adhyaksh / Cyber Cell Incharge, [Police Station / Cyber Cell Name], [City] Vishay: Online Fraud / Dhokhadhadi dwara paise thagne ke sambandh me. Mahoday, Savinay nivedan hai ki mera naam [Aapka Naam] hai. Mera Bank Account [Bank Name], Account No: [Account Number] hai. Dinaank [Date] ko mujhe ek unknown number [Scammer ka Mobile Number] se call/message aaya. Unhone mujhe [Kaaran, jaise: Job Offer / Lottery / Fake Scheme] ka laalach dekar mere sath ₹[Rashi] ki dhokhadhadi kar li. Transaction ki details: 1. Transaction ID: [UPI / Bank Reference No] 2. Kitna Paisa Gaya: ₹[Amount] 3. Jis Account me Gaya: [Aropi ka UPI/Bank Detail] Atah aapse prarthna hai ki is mamle ki janch karke mere paise wapas karwane aur aropi par kanooni karwai karne ki kripa karein. Dhananyawad. Aavedak, Naam: [Aapka Naam] Mobile: [Mobile Number] Dinaank: [Date]

Mobile Phone Loss Complaint

Mobile kho jaane par police report ka format.

Seva me, Shri maan Thana Adhyaksh Ji, [Police Station ka Naam], [City Name] Vishay: Mobile Phone kho jaane ke sambandh me application. Mahoday, Nivedan hai ki mera naam [Aapka Naam] hai. Main [Aapka Pata] ka niwasi hoon. Dinaank [Tareekh] ko samay lagbhag [Samay] baje mera mobile phone [Location] ke paas kho gaya hai. Kaafi khojbeen ke baad bhi nahi mila. Mobile details: 1. Brand & Model: [Samsung / iPhone / Redmi] 2. Mobile Number: [SIM Number] 3. IMEI Number: [IMEI Number] Atah aapse nivedan hai ki kripya meri yeh shikayat darj karne ki kripa karein taaki iska misuse na ho. Dhananyawad. Bhavdiya, Naam: [Aapka Naam] Mobile: [Mobile Number] Dinaank: [Date]

2. Banking & Revenue Applications

Bank Account Branch Transfer Application

Account ek branch se doosri branch transfer karwane ka application.

Seva me, Shri maan Shaakha Prabandhak (Branch Manager), [Bank Name], [Current Branch Name] Vishay: Bank Account doosri Branch me Transfer karwane hetu. Mahoday, Savinay nivedan hai ki mera aapki branch me bachat khata (Savings Account) hai, jiska account number [Account Number] hai. Mera niwas sthan badal jaane ke kaaran main apna khata aapki dusri branch [Nayi Branch Name & IFSC Code] me transfer karwana chahta hoon. Khate ki jankari: Khata Dharak ka Naam: [Aapka Naam] Account Number: [Account Number] Nayi Branch Code/IFSC: [IFSC Code] Atah aapse nivedan hai ki mere khate ko jald se jald nayi branch me transfer karne ki kripa karein. Dhananyawad. Bhavdiya, Naam: [Aapka Naam] Mobile: [Mobile Number] Dinaank: [Date]
