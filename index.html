1. Mobile Khone Ki Police FIR / Complaint Application

Police station me khoye hue mobile phone ki soochna dene ke liye application format.

Seva me, Shri maan Thana Adhyaksh Ji, [Police Station ka Naam], [City Name] Vishay: Mobile Phone kho jaane ke sambandh me application. Mahoday, Nivedan hai ki mera naam [Aapka Naam] hai. Main [Aapka Pura Pata] ka niwasi hoon. Dinaank [Tareekh] ko samay lagbhag [Samay] baje mera mobile phone [Jagah ka naam] ke paas kho gaya hai. Kaafi khojbeen ke baad bhi mobile nahi mila. Mobile ki details niche di gayi hain: 1. Mobile Brand & Model: [Jaise: Samsung Galaxy M31] 2. Mobile Number (SIM): [Aapka Number] 3. IMEI Number 1: [15 Digit IMEI] 4. IMEI Number 2: [15 Digit IMEI] Atah aapse nivedan hai ki kripya meri yeh shikayat darj karne ki kripa karein taaki iska galat istemal na ho sake. Dhananyawad. Bhavdiya, Naam: [Aapka Naam] Hastekshya: [Aapki Signature] Phone Number: [Aapka Dusra Number] Pata: [Aapka Pata] Dinaank: [Today Date]

2. RTO / DL Loss Application Format

Driving License kho jaane par Duplicate DL ya FIR ke liye application format.

Seva me, Shri maan Thana Adhyaksh Ji, [Police Station Name], [City] Vishay: Driving License kho jaane ki Soochna hetu. Mahoday, Savinay nivedan hai ki mera naam [Aapka Naam] hai. Mera Original Driving License (DL Number: [DL Number]) dinaank [Tareekh] ko [Location] ke paas kahin gir gaya hai. Atah aapse prarthna hai ki kripya iski missing report darj karke mujhe Ukt Report ki copy pradaan karein taaki main RTO se duplicate DL issue karwa sakoon. Dhananyawad. Naam: [Aapka Naam] Mobile: [Mobile Number] Dinaank: [Date]
